---



Every time a new model comes out these days, People on Twitter go and one-shot (allegedly) a sloppy game no one would play for more than 30 seconds, Building Horse tinder/ Uber but for dogs.



Or these days, the three.js 3d model and maybe computer usage.



It's a horrible way to judge how good a model is. Genuinely, the only thing you get out of these posts is "okay cool, it's better ig."



---



Day-to-day AI usage, there aren't many use cases where you will be one-shotting stuff.

Maybe for when you want to prototype a bunch of solutions. In cases other than when you want to throw the thing away after you view it, one-shotting is a pretty useless way to see how capable the model is.



How many attempts did it take? How much debugging was involved? Can you change the requirements without having to start over? How good was it at diagnosing a bug without tripping over? What is the quality of code that the agent is producing? Can it work within an existing codebase without breaking unrelated stuff?



These are just way more interesting questions that I want answered after a new model comes out than if it can make a submarine game.



With the use of AI, the thing you would be saving is the effort and time of implementation. It also improves the speed of Ideation. A one-shot slop game provides no insights into any of that.



In practice, the best speed and quality I've seen come from AI-assisted programs where you understand what's going on and can bring new ideas to the implementation. That is a lot of iterative work to get good.



---



I don't have a clear solution for this; I mean, slop is just easier to produce, and slop will win on social media. Good things just take more effort and more time, and that doesn't correlate to social media clicks, so you just don't have these good sources being elevated as much.



The point tho, we should stop taking these demos that come out in the first 24 hours of the new model coming out with any bit of seriousness. With the amount of engagement that shi gets, there is at least one person who thinks these demos are an indicator of how good AI is.
